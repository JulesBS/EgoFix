# EgoFix Content Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate every fix before it ships. Score 1-5 on the five ingredients. Minimum bar: 4/5.

## The Five Ingredients

### 1. Specific Trigger
Does the fix name a specific moment the user can identify in real time?
- 5: "Next time you open Instagram and feel the pang..."
- 3: "When you compare yourself to others..."
- 1: "Don't compare yourself"

### 2. Concrete Action
Is the action observable, completable, and unambiguous?
- 5: "Give your actual answer within 3 seconds. No 'I'm easy.'"
- 3: "Share your real opinion"
- 1: "Be more authentic"

### 3. Observation Hook
Does the fix tell the user what to NOTICE? (This is where self-awareness lives.)
- 5: "Notice the first thing your mind does when plans change. Not the second thing — the first flash."
- 3: "Pay attention to how you feel"
- 1: (missing — most weak fixes lack this entirely)

**Type-specific observations:**
- `standard/reversal`: observe behavioral patterns, social dynamics
- `counter`: observe frequency, triggers, timing
- `journal`: observe narrative patterns, stories you tell
- `observation`: observe external behavior in yourself or others
- `body`: observe physical sensations (ONLY body-type — don't add somatic hooks to other types)
- `predict`: observe gap between expectation and reality
- `audit`: observe patterns across the day

### 4. Constraint That Reveals
Does a limitation make the pattern visible?
- 5: "Zero corrections until 6pm — in person, in chat, in email."
- 3: "Try not to correct people"
- 1: "Be patient"

### 5. Inline Comment That Reframes
Does the comment name the MECHANISM, not motivate?
- 5: "The 24 hours isn't about trust. It's about discovering what your anxiety does when it can't check."
- 3: "The discomfort is part of the process."
- 1: "You've got this!" / "Awareness precedes change."

## Scoring

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 5 | All five ingredients. Creates genuine insight. | Ship it. |
| 4 | Four ingredients. Strong but missing one dimension. | Ship it, note the gap. |
| 3 | Three ingredients. Functional but not transformative. | Rewrite before shipping. |
| 2 | Two ingredients. Textbook level. | Complete rewrite needed. |
| 1 | One ingredient. Placeholder. | Delete and start over. |

**Minimum bar: 4/5.**
